How to Play

🎯 Getting Started

  • Choose a bet Amount from the top bar.
  • Pick a number from the grid below — your selection is highlighted in blue.
  • Tap Bet in the popup to confirm and place your bet.

📋 Bet Rules

  • You can only have one active bet at a time for each amount.
  • To change your number or amount, first Cancel Bet using the button in the popup.
  • Numbers are split across 3 pages: 1–100, 101–200, 201–300.

⏱ Countdown Timer

  • The timer at the top counts down the remaining time for the current round.
  • Place your bet before the timer reaches 00:00.

💰 Balance & Payouts

  • Your current balance is shown in the top-left corner.
  • Winnings are calculated based on the amount you selected.

🆔 Account

  • Your account is identified by your phone number.
  • Open the app with the URL containing token, phone, username, and balance params.
